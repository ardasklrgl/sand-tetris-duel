About the letters

The scene is built from the project's README, flattened into a continuous paragraph. Each letter stays in place while its brightness follows your beach video. The scene uses purple shades with yellow highlights, and the two hues slowly shift together. Color threshold controls where highlights turn yellow. Enlarge the letters to read the texture.
